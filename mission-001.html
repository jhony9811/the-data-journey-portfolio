Sales Intelligence

An analytical mission focused on understanding sales performance, identifying trends and discovering opportunities for business growth.

01

Business Problem

What is happening with sales performance?

The mission begins by identifying the business context, the central challenge and the objective of the analysis.

01

Business context

A business has sales information distributed across multiple products, customers, locations and periods. However, raw records alone do not clearly explain business performance.

02

Main challenge

Management needs a clear view of total sales, profitability, product performance and customer behaviour to support better decisions.

03

Analytical objective

Transform the available sales data into reliable indicators, visualizations and practical business recommendations.

02

Key Questions

Questions guiding the investigation.

These questions will guide the cleaning, analysis and visualization of the sales information.

01

What is the total sales revenue generated during the analyzed period?

02

Which products and categories generate the highest sales?

03

Which periods show the strongest and weakest sales performance?

04

Which customers or market segments contribute most to revenue?

05

Which regions or locations generate the highest sales?

06

What opportunities exist to improve sales and profitability?

03

Dataset

Information powering the mission.

This section will document the source, structure and quality of the dataset selected for the project.

SOURCE

Data origin

The sales dataset will contain transactional information about orders, products, customers, locations, quantities, prices and dates.

FORMAT

File structure

The initial file will be reviewed in Excel before being prepared for analysis and visualization in Power BI.

STATUS

Dataset selection

The final dataset is currently being selected and will be published here after its variables and quality are verified.

04

Analytical Process

Mission execution plan.

The project documents every stage from dataset inspection to business recommendations.

01

Data Collection

Review the sales dataset

Identify available variables, file formats, time periods and business dimensions.

02

Data Cleaning

Prepare reliable information

Correct missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formats and other data quality problems.

03

Data Transformation

Create analysis-ready variables

Standardize dates, calculate sales values, organize categories and prepare the data model.

04

Data Analysis

Calculate business indicators

Measure total sales, product performance, customer contribution, regional results and sales trends.

05

Visualization

Build an interactive dashboard

Present the results through indicators, charts, tables and filters using Power BI.

06

Recommendations

Convert findings into actions

Propose practical business decisions based on the strongest patterns discovered in the data.

05

Performance Indicators

Metrics planned for the dashboard.

KPI 01

Total sales

Total monetary value generated by all completed sales transactions.

KPI 02

Total orders

Number of sales orders registered during the selected period.

KPI 03

Average order value

Average revenue generated by each sales order.

KPI 04

Units sold

Total number of product units sold during the analyzed period.

KPI 05

Top product

Product responsible for generating the highest sales value.

KPI 06

Sales growth

Percentage change in sales performance between comparable periods.
